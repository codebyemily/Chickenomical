## 1. Executive summary

This plan optimizes for one outcome: **prove that a trustworthy local chicken price comparison earns repeat use in a single launch geography** — before paid spend, before nationwide claims, and before monetization experiments that would undermine trust.

### Three big bets

1. **Product-as-proof is the marketing.** Until one area has real, attributable pickup prices for a small set of chains, every acquisition channel is theater. Marketing spend and content only unlock after the Phase 0 ingestion gate in the product brief is green.
2. **Earn attention with inspectable math, not savings theater.** Default public promise: *compare same-size nugget/tender offers nearby by price per piece* (aligned with the prototype headline). Hold or kill “up to 89% off” until a dated, reproducible baseline exists (§4, §13).
3. **Compounding portfolio at Tier 1 budget.** Stack three cheap assets that reuse the same proof: (a) one local SEO landing page for a real query in the launch area, (b) one short-form “4pc vs 4pc” demo clip into search, (c) a shareable comparison link after first value — no rewards until organic share proves out.

### Twelve months, plausibly

- One primary metro (or demo radius) with reliable coverage for 3–5 chains; freshness and source always visible.
- Organic acquisition from local search + founder-led short form; measurable “search → completed comparison” rate.
- Soft retention via recent-search UX; soft referral via share links with stale-price handling.
- Monetization decision made with a trust-safe model (not ads inside rankings); unit economics known enough to set CAC rules.
- Delivery-platform / checkout-total compare still optional stretch — only if same-basket data quality holds.

### 90-day priorities

1. Lock launch geography + chain list; ship attributable prices (product gate).
2. Publish positioning one-liner and landing promise (no unverified % savings).
3. Instrument north-star: **completed comparisons in covered area** (and leading indicators in §13).
4. Ship one local SEO page once live offers exist.
5. Ship one short-form walkthrough of same-size compare → search.
6. Decide monetization hypothesis for Q2+ (options in §8); defer paid ads.

## 2. Strategic frame

### What Chickenomical is, in one sentence

**Chickenomical shows where the closest, cheapest chicken is** — same-size nugget and tender offers nearby, with total price and price per piece you can verify.

### Category claim

We are not “another food delivery app.” We are a **local fast-food price comparison utility** (starting with chicken portions). Competitors are mental math, chain apps, and delivery app browsing — not DoorDash as a peer unless/until we compare checkout totals honestly.

### Who we’re for (ICP)

- Value-conscious diners about to buy nuggets or tenders nearby (pickup).
- People ordering for more than one person who need “at least N pieces” without overpaying.
- Careful shoppers who want source + “checked at” before they trust a deal.
- **Not yet:** nationwide travelers, delivery-fee optimizers, combo/meal-deal hunters (MVP excludes combos and third-party checkout math).

**Stated want:** cheapest chicken nearby.  
**Actual want:** a fair comparison (like-with-like size, clear unit price, enough pieces) without app-hopping.

### Market-quality gate

| | Low frequency | High frequency |
|---|---|---|
| **Large problem** | Expensive to stay top-of-mind | **Target — if consideration exists** |
| **Small problem** | Weak | Habit/price churn |

Chicken sits near **large × high frequency** for frequent buyers, with a caveat: many purchases are low-consideration. Marketing must create a sharp “aha” (same-size + $/piece) or usage won’t stick. If founders later find users won’t open a tool before ordering, revisit category and ICP before scaling acquisition (§13).

### Business-model logic

**Today:** undefined (correct for MVP).  
**Working rule until decided:** free utility; never sell ranking placement; never invent prices.  
**Likely later paths (ranked by trust fit):** affiliate/continue-to-order when channel compare exists; optional alerts/pro features; B2B data only if privacy and ToS allow. **Ads in results are incompatible with the trust promise.**

### Brand voice (non-negotiable)

- **Direct, inspectable, slightly sharp** — math users can check.
- YES: price per piece, same-size, source, checked-at, straight-line distance (labeled), coverage honesty.
- NO: unverified % savings, “guaranteed cheapest,” fake freshness, mixing nuggets/tenders or combos into unit rankings, calling straight-line “drive time.”
- CTA: invite to compare — don’t pressure checkout; we don’t place the order.

## 4. Acquisition

**Job:** Strangers → aware that Chickenomical compares nearby chicken by piece.

### Current
None live. Drafted hooks in user stories: Easy Keyword Ranking (#1), Short Form Video (#42), Viral Loops (#93) as referral-adjacent.

### Plan (Tier 1 only)

1. **Coverage-gated local SEO** — After live offers: one page for a real query (e.g. “4-piece nugget prices near [launch area]”) with attributable, timestamped prices or clear empty/stale states. Skill: `programmatic-seo` / `seo-audit` later; start manual.
2. **One short-form proof clip** — Screen capture of same-size 4pc vs 4pc → sort by $/piece → open source. Link to search. Kill if it doesn’t drive completed comparisons.
3. **Founder-led social** — Post the clip + “how we calculate” thread; no daily content calendar until retention exists.
4. **Hackathon / demo as earned** — Demo video and sponsor evidence double as launch assets; don’t build a separate brand campaign.
5. **Skip for now:** Paid social/search, influencer retainers, broad content blog, App Store scale spend, HARO spam, “89%” performance creatives.

### 90-day moves
Ship (1) and (2) only after product gate; instrument visits → comparison start.

### 12-month outlook
If local SEO converts, expand to more size×area pages. If short-form converts, systematize monthly clips. Paid only after CAC can be defined (§8, seed unlock §10).

### Skills + tools
`ai-seo`, `programmatic-seo`, `copywriting`, `launch`; analytics (GA4 or PostHog) when public URL exists.

## 5. Activation

**Job:** Aware → first valued experience (completed comparison they trust).

### Current
Prototype: location/area → category → sort (unit / total / distance) → offer detail / source. Empty/stale states specified in stories US-07/US-08.

### Plan

1. **Promise match on entry** — Landing/store: nearby brand compare, $/piece, coverage honesty; primary CTA to location + category (US-08).
2. **Time-to-aha &lt; 60 seconds** — Minimize steps to first ranked same-size list with total + unit price.
3. **Trust chrome always on** — Source, checked-at, freshness labels; never fill gaps with invented prices (US-06/US-09).
4. **Coverage outside area** — Explicit “not covered” path; capture waitlist email only if team will use it (optional).
5. **Defer:** accounts, paywalls, heavy onboarding carousels.

### 90-day moves
Polish empty/stale/unsupported states; align public copy with prototype voice (“Find a better price per piece”).

### Skills + tools
`onboarding`, `copywriting`; product analytics event: `comparison_completed`.

## 6. Retention

**Job:** Repeat useful searches without redoing setup; never show stale prices as current.

### Current
US-11 (recent search) — later.

### Plan

1. **Recent search / last settings** — Local-only OK at first; prices always re-fetched or clearly re-checked.
2. **No fake push urgency** — If push/email ever exists, only for user-requested alerts (e.g. price drop) after monetization model allows.
3. **Skip until product stickiness:** full lifecycle email suite, win-back campaigns, loyalty.

### 90-day moves
Ship lightweight recent-search if engineering bandwidth allows after core compare; otherwise Q2.

### Skills + tools

## 7. Referral

**Job:** Retained users bring friends via useful shares — not coupon spam.

### Current

## 8. Revenue

**Job:** Monetize without corrupting rankings.

### Current
Explicitly open in user stories — keep it that way until proof.

### Options (decision needed — §13)

| Model | Fit | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Free + later affiliate “continue to order” | High when channel compare exists | ToS / attribution complexity |
| Pro alerts (price drop / weekly digest) | Medium | Needs accounts + retention |
| Sponsored placements | **Reject for core rankings** | Destroys trust |
| Data licensing | Long-shot | Legal / provider ToS |

### 90-day moves
Choose a **hypothesis**, not a launch paywall. Document “what we will never sell.”

### 12-month
If affiliate path: only with same-basket honesty (US-12). Price experiments via `pricing` skill after usage data exists.

### Skills + tools
`pricing`, `paywalls` only after model choice; Stripe later.

## 13. Measurement, RACI, open decisions, appendix

### North-star
**Completed comparisons in a covered area per week** (user ran a search and viewed a ranked comparable list with real offers).

### Leading indicators
| Stage | Indicator |
|---|---|
| Acquisition | Landing/SEO visits; clip view → site/app open |
| Activation | Location set → comparison completed; empty-state rate |
| Retention | 7-day return to search (when measurable) |
| Referral | Shares → fresh searches |
| Revenue | TBD after model choice |
| Trust | % results with fresh source; stale shown as stale |

### RACI (until team named)
| Work | R | A | C | I |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Product proof / data | Eng | Founder | — | Team |
| Public copy / claims | Founder | Founder | Eng | — |
| SEO page + video | Founder | Founder | Eng | — |
| Analytics | Eng | Founder | — | — |
| Monetization decision | Founder | Founder | — | Team |

### Open decisions (ranked by impact)

1. **Launch geography and chain list** — blocks all acquisition content.
2. **Monetization hypothesis** — blocks Revenue § and CAC math.
3. **Funding / budget confirmation** — validates Tier 1 assumptions.
4. **Team marketing owner** — RACI names.
5. **Whether to pursue any numeric savings claim** — if yes, commission baseline study; if no, delete 89% from all drafts.
6. **Public surface** — web-first vs Expo app store path for first users.
7. **Market-quality check** — after 50–100 users, do they open the tool *before* ordering?
